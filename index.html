Video Creator

Video Creator is a personal, open-source "video-as-code" tool. It renders 60-second technical explainer videos with Manim, edge-tts and ffmpeg, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

The application is used only by its developer to publish videos to a channel they own. It is not offered to the public as a service and does not process any other person's data.
